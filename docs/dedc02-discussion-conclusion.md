# Discussion and Conclusion lesson

Route: `/teaching/courses/dedc02/10-discussion-conclusion/`.

The 140-minute session has 20 minutes on Results, 100 on Discussion (including limitations, contribution and implications), and 20 on Conclusion. The reference slides are reading resources, not an additional teaching block. There are no breaks or slide footers. Shared navigation remains in the established lesson chrome.

The transferable content is the movement from an analytical finding to a substantive interpretation; developing and checking explanations; connecting findings to theoretical propositions; explaining relationships with previous research; integrating findings and handling contradictory evidence; deriving limitations, contribution and implications; and synthesising the whole argument in a Conclusion.

The six Discussion tasks and classroom worksheets are a teaching synthesis. Do not attribute that complete framework to one textbook. Miles and colleagues' qualitative analytic tactics must be adapted to the actual design and epistemology; they are not a universal quantitative validity checklist.

## Empirical examples

- Deslauriers et al. (2019), DOI `10.1073/pnas.1821936116`: distinguish perceived learning from measured learning in the investigated physics setting. No numerical effect has been invented or redrawn.
- Carless (2020), DOI `10.1080/07294360.2019.1684455`: four undergraduate cases, five-year qualitative inquiry, repeated interviews and documents; academic-adviser relationship. The negative-case exercise is explicitly constructed and is not an article quotation or a reported participant.
- Mueller & Oppenheimer (2014), DOI `10.1177/0956797614524581`, paired with Urry et al. (2021), DOI `10.1177/0956797620965541`: retain the distinction between note characteristics and immediate quiz outcomes. The replication is of Study 1; nonsignificance alone is not equivalence.

Published records, author-hosted full texts and the HKU accepted manuscript were inspected during preparation. The APA, USC and Sacred Heart guides are supplementary writing resources, not the methodological framework.

## Constructed material and response handling

The running EFL feedback study (120 learners, 48/72 gain patterns, 12 interviewees), its learner accounts, all weak/strong paragraph pairs and model conclusions are constructed teaching material. They are not real project data or quotations from published authors. The one-point split is an illustrative sampling choice, not a validated threshold. No theory citation has been fabricated to complete a model paragraph.

Responses, choices and review checks use browser-local storage under `dedc02-discussion-conclusion-v1`. English/Traditional Chinese views share response values without translating the student's writing. Storage failure is disclosed beside the activities. Markdown export includes the current responses and checked explanations; no data are submitted to a server. Existing lesson storage keys are unchanged.

The website contains bibliographic references and public article links. Copyrighted textbook PDFs and private vault files are not uploaded.
