## gaming-apartment

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape teaching slide image, 16:9 composition. Primary request: a futuristic gaming apartment interior for university General English students to describe. A spacious room with a large desktop gaming setup, two monitors showing abstract game landscapes without text, comfortable chair, sofa, modern shelves and a broad window overlooking an evening city. Stylised architectural visualisation, carefully designed, credible interior proportions, rich teal and soft amber lighting, lots of clearly recognisable objects and room details. Full-frame room, wide eye-level view. No people, no words, no logos, no watermark. Clearly an imagined room, not an actual person's home.
