## Reading the Language Testing resource page

Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/page/ltj/reviewer_resources

The Test Review checklist concerns manuscripts evaluating language tests. It should not become a universal requirement for every research article. The AI guidance governs confidential reviewing. The three public review exemplars make reviewing decisions visible across different article types. Teaching analysis should distinguish what a source reports from the lecturer’s evaluation of that report.

## Retained comparison: vague and actionable criticism

This is an original teaching illustration, separate from the Burton study.

**Vague:** “The methodology is weak and the analysis is not rigorous. Please improve it.”

**Context:** A survey measures willingness to use AI, but the conclusion describes actual uptake. No usage records were collected.

**Actionable:** In the Discussion, the conclusion that students frequently used AI is based on a willingness scale, not observed use. This conflates intention with behaviour. Please revise the outcome description throughout the manuscript to willingness to use AI, explain this measurement limitation, and remove claims about actual uptake unless independent usage evidence is available.

**中文說明：**討論部分以使用意願量表支持學生經常使用AI的結論，混淆了意圖與行為。請把全文的結果描述修訂為使用AI的意願，交代測量限制；如沒有獨立的使用證據，應刪除有關實際使用的主張。

The improvement comes from identifying the mismatch and its consequence, then specifying a revision that existing evidence can support. The example explains the method; it is not a drafting assignment.

## Assessing a report

A useful report represents the study accurately, ties concerns to evidence, explains consequences, specifies proportionate changes, puts central concerns first and treats authors fairly. A recommendation follows from the seriousness of the concerns and whether revision can resolve them. A count of negative comments is not a decision rule.

複審應對照作者回覆與修訂文稿，判斷原問題是否已處理。有理據的替代回應可以接受；不以逐項服從為標準。
