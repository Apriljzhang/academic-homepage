Formative assessment and technology support

Annotated reading appendix · AI4ELE / TIRF workshop · April Jiawei Zhang.

Readings on formative assessment, feedback and technology in language education.

Read with these distinctions

  • Informing-only examples need purposeful teacher/learner action intended to support development and a proportionate check of later evidence.
  • Digital assessment is not automatically AI-assisted or formative; pre-GenAI studies do not test Codex or WorkBuddy.
  • Effects depend on context, implementation and what is measured. Do not rank review effect sizes or promise doubled learning.
  • The three workshop frameworks are Black and Wiliam (2009), Hattie and Timperley (2007), and Gu (2021).
