Two English-class scenarios in China中國英語課堂的兩個情境

1 · Primary English in China一 · 中國小學英語

Grade 4, about 40 pupils; one projector. Pupils know some animal words but need help reading and making sentences. Explain in Chinese, read the English aloud and accept pointing or a Chinese explanation.四年級，約40人，一部投影機。學生認識部分動物詞彙，但閱讀及造句仍需協助。用中文解說、朗讀英語，接受指認或中文解釋。

Learning target學習目標

Use “It has ...” for a body feature and “It can ...” for an action.用“It has ...”說身體特徵，用“It can ...”說動作。

Student task and evidence學生任務與證據

Show a rabbit with long ears, jumping. With “long ears / jump” provided, each pupil chooses has or can, explains why and writes or says the sentences. Paper and oral answers work here.展示一隻長耳朵、正在跳的兔子，提供“long ears / jump”。每人選has或can、說明理由，再寫出或說出句子，可用紙本及口頭作答。

It can long ears. It has jump.

Teacher check: does the pupil understand the words? Can they distinguish a body feature from an action? Use the answer to choose feedback, then read or hear the revision.教師檢視：學生是否理解詞彙？能否分清身體特徵與動作？依作答選擇回饋，再看或聽修訂。

2 · General college English in China二 · 中國高校大學英語

The students and the teaching problem學生與教學問題

A first-year general college English class in China. Students read short texts with help and need support to explain a reason in English. Explain the task in Chinese; allow Chinese or mixed-language reasons before a short English revision. Provide key words and the sentence opening “This helps us ... because ...”.中國高校非英語專業一年級大學英語班。學生需要協助閱讀短文及用英語解釋理由。任務用中文解說，先接受中文或中英夾雜的理由，再用簡短英語修訂。提供關鍵詞及句首“This helps us ... because ...”。

What students are learning to do學生要學會做甚麼

Students can support an opinion with a specific, relevant example and explain how it supports their point.學生能以具體相關的例子支持觀點，並解釋例子如何支持自己的看法。

What the teacher and students will look for教師與學生會看甚麼These questions show students what good work looks like. They give the teacher and students the same focus when discussing feedback and revisions.這兩條問題讓學生知道作品要做到甚麼，也讓教師與學生在討論回饋及修訂時看同樣的重點。

  • Is the example specific and relevant to the opinion?例子是否具體，並與觀點相關？
  • Does the writer explain how it supports the opinion?作者有否解釋例子如何支持觀點？

The task you give students你給學生的任務

“Which paragraph supports the opinion more clearly? Explain why. Then improve A in your own words.”「哪一段更清楚地支持觀點？請解釋原因，再用自己的話改寫A。」

Opinion: 觀點：Students should have a short reading period every school day.

Paragraph A段落A

Reading every day is good. It is useful and important. Students will become better because reading helps them.

Paragraph B段落B

Reading a little English every day can help us learn words. For example, we can read one short text every morning. We see the same words again, so we can remember them better.

Purpose of feedback回饋的目的

Help close the gap between present understanding or performance and the learning goal.協助縮小差距，讓目前理解或表現更接近學習目標。

Learners’ responses學習者的回應

Learners may use a better strategy or invest purposeful effort. They may also give up, obscure the goal or settle for less; those responses can reduce the perceived gap without improving learning.學習者可以採用更合適的方法或投入有方向的努力，也可能放棄、模糊目標或降低要求；後者可減少感受到的差距，卻未必促進學習。

Teachers’ support教師的支持

Set clear, suitably challenging goals. Help learners reach them through strategies, instruction and feedback.訂立清晰且具適當挑戰的目標，以策略、教學與回饋支持學生達標。

Each question can address four levels每個問題均可涉及四個層面

Task任務層面

Information about the quality or accuracy of this response.針對這次作答的質素或正確性。

“Your paragraph gives an example, but does not explain its link to the opinion.”「你的段落有例子，但未解釋它與觀點的聯繫。」

Process過程層面

Information about a strategy for approaching the work.針對完成任務所用的方法。

“Ask what the example makes possible. Use that answer to connect it to your opinion.”「問自己：這個例子能帶來甚麼？用答案連結你的觀點。」

Self-regulation自我調節層面

Support for checking, directing and adjusting one’s own learning.支持學生監察、引導及調整自己的學習。

Self自我／個人層面

Judgements about the person, often with little information for improving the work.對個人的評價，往往缺乏有助改進作品的資訊。

Keep the same learning target when collecting responses, interpreting them, giving feedback and deciding action. Peers and learners participate in this process, and the feedback questions help shape the information they use.蒐集作答、詮釋、給予回饋及決定行動時，均以同一目標為參照。同伴與學生參與此過程，回饋問題則幫助整理他們可運用的資訊。

Learning target學習目標

Eliciting evidence蒐集證據

Create an opportunity to reveal learning against the target.創造機會，讓學生呈現與目標相關的學習表現。

Ask everyone to choose A or B, explain why and improve A. The reason “because it is longer” adds information that the correct choice alone cannot provide.要求每人選擇A或B、解釋原因，再改寫A。「因為較長」透露的資訊，單靠選對答案無法得知。

Interpreting evidence詮釋證據

Judge what the response suggests about the target; consider other explanations.判斷作答透露了哪些與目標相關的資訊，並考慮其他解釋。

The learner may be using length as a shortcut. Check with equally long examples before concluding that they cannot judge relevance; the instructions may also need clarification.學生可能以長度作捷徑。先比較長度相同的例子，再判斷他是否未能辨別相關性；指示亦可能需要澄清。

Formative feedback形成性回饋

Use that interpretation to offer information that supports progress.依據詮釋，提供有助進步的資訊。

After the learner writes, ask: “What makes your example relevant to this opinion?” If the link is missing, prompt an explanation rather than supplying a finished paragraph.學生寫作後，問：「你的例子為何與這個觀點相關？」若欠缺聯繫，就提示他解釋，讓學生自行完成段落。

Learner/teacher take-up and action學習者／教師採納與行動

Learners and/or teachers act on the feedback to support learning.學生及／或教師依回饋採取行動，以支持學習。

The learner revises and explains the change. The teacher may model a link, give targeted practice or extend the task. Read the revision to decide what support is still needed.學生修訂並解釋改動。教師可示範聯繫、提供針對練習或延伸任務，再依修訂判斷仍需要甚麼協助。

How to read purpose, practice and effect如何理解目的、實踐與效果

Purpose: what learning do we want to support?目的：我們想支持哪方面的學習？
In this class, we want students to explain how an example supports their opinion. This gives the assessment its direction.在本課堂中，我們希望學生能解釋例子如何支持觀點，這為評估提供方向。
Practice: what do we do with the evidence?實踐：我們怎樣運用證據？
Read the first draft, interpret what it shows, give feedback and let the student revise. The teacher follows up where help is still needed.閱讀初稿、詮釋表現、給予回饋，並讓學生修訂；教師再按需要跟進。
Effect: what progress can we find?效果：我們能看到甚麼進步？
Compare the first draft and revision against the same target. Ask the student why they made the change. Use the explanation to judge whether they understand the connection and whether more help is needed.依同一目標比較初稿與修訂，再問學生為何修改。運用解釋判斷他是否理解聯繫，以及仍需甚麼協助。

Use the site for formative assessment用網站進行形成性評估

  1. Collect the first response蒐集初次作答

  2. Interpret before giving feedback先詮釋，再給回饋

    Read or listen to the reason. Check vocabulary or the instructions if needed. Choose feedback that addresses the learning target.看或聽理由，有需要時核對詞彙或指示的理解，再依學習目標選擇回饋。

  3. Let students use the feedback讓學生運用回饋

    A partner asks a question. The writer revises, checks the work and explains a change; younger pupils can point or explain in Chinese.同伴提問，作者修訂、自查並解釋修改；小學生可指認或用中文說明。

  4. Use the revision to decide the next step依修訂決定下一步

    Compare the first and revised responses with the same target. Ask a follow-up question or give more support where needed. Collect paper answers or exported records from devices.依同一目標比較初稿與修訂，有需要時追問或提供協助，再收取紙本或各裝置匯出的記錄。
