Workshop purpose工作坊目的

Understand formative assessment, then use the three frameworks to design a classroom activity and feedback with generative AI.理解形成性評估，再依三個框架運用生成式AI設計課堂活動與回饋。

The shared classroom case共同課堂案例

Workshop role: you are the teacher designing this lesson. The English writing below is for your students.工作坊角色：你是設計本課堂的教師。以下英語寫作是給你的學生做的。

The students and the teaching problem學生與教學問題

Imagine an intermediate English class. Students can state an opinion, but often use vague examples or do not explain why an example supports the opinion. You want to help them develop that explanation.設想一個中級英語班。學生能表達觀點，但例子常不夠具體，或沒有解釋例子為何支持觀點。你希望幫助他們把解釋寫清楚。

What students are learning to do學生要學會做甚麼

Students can support an opinion with a specific, relevant example and explain how it supports their point.學生能以具體相關的例子支持觀點，並解釋例子如何支持自己的看法。

What the teacher and students will look for教師與學生會看甚麼These questions show students what good work looks like. They give the teacher and students the same focus when discussing feedback and revisions.這兩條問題讓學生知道作品要做到甚麼，也讓教師與學生在討論回饋及修訂時看同樣的重點。

  • Is the example specific and relevant to the opinion?例子是否具體，並與觀點相關？
  • Does the writer explain how it supports the opinion?作者有否解釋例子如何支持觀點？

The task you give students你給學生的任務

“Which paragraph supports the opinion more clearly? Explain why. Then improve A in your own words.”「哪一段更清楚地支持觀點？請解釋原因，再用自己的話改寫A。」

Opinion: 觀點：Students should have a short reading period every school day.

Paragraph A段落A

Reading every day is good. It is useful and important. Students will become better because reading helps them.

Paragraph B段落B

A short reading period can help students build a reading habit. For example, learners could finish one short article each day and record two unfamiliar words. This regular practice would give them repeated opportunities to meet vocabulary in context.

The English paragraphs are student materials and stay in English in both language modes. This is a fictional teaching example.英語段落是學生學習材料，兩種語言模式均保留英語原文。本案例為虛構教學情境。

How the lesson continues課堂如何繼續

  1. Read every student’s reason and first draft to see what needs attention.看每位學生的理由及初稿，了解哪裏需要協助。
  2. Give a focused hint. A partner asks how the example supports the opinion.提供針對性提示，同伴提問例子如何支持觀點。
  3. Students revise, check their own work and explain one change.學生修訂、自查，並解釋一項改動。
  4. Use their responses to decide what to teach next. Later ask them to support a new opinion with less help.依作答決定接下來教甚麼，稍後讓學生在較少協助下支持一個新觀點。
Inspect the student activity here在本頁查看學生活動

This is the student view of the lesson above. As a workshop teacher, you may inspect it or type a sample response to see how revision works.這是上述課堂的學生畫面。你作為工作坊教師，可直接檢視，或輸入模擬作答，觀察修訂如何進行。

Prepared hint預先準備的提示

Ask yourself: what could students practise by reading an article each morning? Use the answer to explain why the example supports daily reading.問自己：學生每天早上讀文章，可以練習甚麼？用答案解釋這個例子為何支持每天閱讀。

The same hint appears for everyone. It has not analysed your writing.每人會看到同一提示；它並沒有分析你的寫作。

Later task, with less help: 稍後在較少協助下完成的任務：Students should sometimes choose what they read.

Self-check: can you point to a relevant example and the words explaining its connection? The teacher reads this new response to decide whether further support is needed.自查：你能否指出相關例子，以及解釋聯繫的文字？教師閱讀這份新作答，再決定是否需要進一步協助。

The core concept核心概念

Teachers and students use evidence of learning to decide what to do next to help students reach a learning target.教師與學生運用學習證據，決定接下來怎樣教與學，以幫助學生達到學習目標。

“B, because it is longer” gives you more to work with than “B. Correct.” Check what the student understands, then use that information in feedback and teaching.「B，因為較長」比「B。答對了」提供更多可用資訊。先了解學生理解了甚麼，再據此給予回饋及教學。

Purpose of feedback回饋的目的

Help close the gap between present understanding or performance and the learning goal.協助縮小目前理解或表現與學習目標之間的差距。

Learners’ responses學習者的回應

Learners may use a better strategy or invest purposeful effort. They may also give up, obscure the goal or settle for less; those responses can reduce the perceived gap without improving learning.學習者可以採用更合適的方法或投入有方向的努力，也可能放棄、模糊目標或降低要求；後者可減少感受到的差距，卻未必促進學習。

Teachers’ support教師的支持

Set clear, suitably challenging goals. Help learners reach them through strategies, instruction and feedback.訂立清晰且具適當挑戰的目標，以策略、教學與回饋支持學生達標。

Three feedback questions三個回饋問題

Feed up釐清目標

Where am I going?我要達到甚麼？

Clarify the goal and what meeting it looks like.釐清目標，以及達標的具體表現。

“Support your opinion with a specific relevant example and explain the connection.”「以具體相關的例子支持觀點，並解釋兩者的聯繫。」

Feed back檢視目前表現

How am I going?我目前的表現如何？

Locate current performance in relation to that goal.依目標判斷目前的表現。

“Your daily-reading example is specific. The reader still needs to know how it supports your opinion.”「你每天閱讀的例子很具體，但讀者仍需知道它如何支持你的觀點。」

Feed forward指向下一步

Where to next?下一步做甚麼？

Identify a productive way to make further progress.指出有助繼續進步的方法。

“Add a sentence explaining what regular reading makes possible; then check the link.”「加一句解釋經常閱讀能帶來甚麼，再檢查它與觀點的聯繫。」

Each question can address four levels每個問題均可涉及四個層面

Task任務層面

Information about the quality or accuracy of this response.針對這次作答的質素或正確性。

“Your paragraph gives an example, but does not explain its link to the opinion.”「你的段落有例子，但未解釋它與觀點的聯繫。」
Process過程層面

Information about a strategy for approaching the work.針對完成任務所用的方法。

“Ask what the example makes possible. Use that answer to connect it to your opinion.”「問自己：這個例子能帶來甚麼？用答案連結你的觀點。」
Self-regulation自我調節層面

Support for checking, directing and adjusting one’s own learning.支持學生監察、引導及調整自己的學習。

“Check your example and explanation yourself. Which part needs revision, and what help would you choose?”「自行檢查例子及解釋。哪一部分需要修訂？你會選擇甚麼協助？」
Self自我／個人層面

Judgements about the person, often with little information for improving the work.對個人的評價，往往缺乏有助改進作品的資訊。

“You are a brilliant writer!” gives no guidance for adding the missing explanation.「你是出色的作者！」未能指導學生補上欠缺的解釋。

Choose the focus that helps this learner progress. The levels are different feedback targets, rather than a four-step sequence; person-focused praise usually offers little guidance for learning.按學習者的需要選擇回饋焦點。四個層面指不同回饋對象，並非四個依次進行的步驟；只評價個人的讚賞，通常難以提供學習指引。

The learning target guides evidence, interpretation, feedback and student/teacher action. Purpose, practice and learning effect are distinct; progress may require several cycles.學習目標引導證據、詮釋、回饋及學生／教師行動。目的、實踐與學習效果各有意義，進步可能需要多個循環。

Eliciting evidence蒐集證據

Create an opportunity to reveal learning against the target.創造機會，讓學生呈現與目標相關的學習表現。

Ask everyone to choose A or B, explain why and improve A. The reason “because it is longer” adds information that the correct choice alone cannot provide.要求每人選擇A或B、解釋原因，再改寫A。「因為較長」透露的資訊，單靠選對答案無法得知。

AI can help you prepare: AI可協助你準備：Ask AI to draft contrasting examples and a question that requires a reason, not just a choice.請AI草擬對比例子，以及要求說明理由的問題。

Interpreting evidence詮釋證據

Judge what the response suggests about the target; consider other explanations.判斷作答透露了哪些與目標相關的資訊，並考慮其他解釋。

The learner may be using length as a shortcut. Check with equally long examples before concluding that they cannot judge relevance; the instructions may also need clarification.學生可能以長度作捷徑。先比較長度相同的例子，再判斷他是否未能辨別相關性；指示亦可能需要澄清。

AI can help you prepare: AI可協助你準備：Ask AI for possible interpretations of fictional responses and an alternative explanation. Decide which follow-up question you need.請AI提出虛構作答的可能詮釋及另一種解釋，再由你決定需要甚麼追問。

Formative feedback形成性回饋

Use that interpretation to offer information that supports progress.依據詮釋，提供有助進步的資訊。

After the learner writes, ask: “What makes your example relevant to this opinion?” If the link is missing, prompt an explanation rather than supplying a finished paragraph.學生寫作後，問：「你的例子為何與這個觀點相關？」若欠缺聯繫，就提示他解釋，讓學生自行完成段落。

AI can help you prepare: AI可協助你準備：Ask AI for task, process and self-regulation feedback options. Check each against the student’s actual words before choosing.請AI提供任務、過程及自我調節回饋選項，核對學生實際用詞後再選擇。

Learner/teacher take-up and action學習者／教師採納與行動

Learners and/or teachers act on the feedback to support learning.學生及／或教師依回饋採取行動，以支持學習。

The learner revises and explains the change. The teacher may model a link, give targeted practice or extend the task. Check later with a fresh opinion and less support.學生修訂並解釋改動。教師可示範聯繫、提供針對練習或延伸任務，再以新觀點及較少支援檢視表現。

AI can help you prepare: AI可協助你準備：Ask AI to draft a revision step, a teacher follow-up and a new task. Check that students must do something with the feedback.請AI草擬修訂步驟、教師跟進及新任務，確保學生需要運用回饋採取行動。

The complete lesson design完整課堂設計

  1. Learning target學習目標

    Support an opinion with a relevant example and explain the connection. Compare A and B to show what this means.以相關例子支持觀點並解釋聯繫；比較A、B，讓學生明白目標的意思。

    Black & Wiliam: clarify the target; Gu: the target guides every step.Black與Wiliam：釐清目標；Gu：每一步均依目標而定。

  2. Student response and teacher interpretation學生作答與教師詮釋

    Every student chooses a paragraph, explains why and improves A. The teacher checks whether the example is relevant and the connection is explained.每位學生選段落、解釋原因並改寫A。教師檢視例子是否相關，以及是否解釋聯繫。

    Black & Wiliam: reveal understanding; Gu: evidence and interpretation.Black與Wiliam：呈現理解；Gu：證據與詮釋。

  3. Feedback and student action回饋與學生行動

    Use a process hint: “Ask what students could practise by reading an article each morning.” Students revise and explain the change; partners ask how the example supports the opinion.使用過程提示：「問自己，學生每天早上讀文章可以練習甚麼？」學生修訂並解釋改動，同伴提問例子如何支持觀點。

    Hattie & Timperley: process feedback and next step; Black & Wiliam: feedback, peers and ownership.Hattie與Timperley：過程回饋及下一步；Black與Wiliam：回饋、同伴與自主學習。

  4. Teacher follow-up and a new task教師跟進與新任務

    If the link is still missing, model one with a different example. Later ask students to support the opinion about choosing their own reading, with less help.若仍欠聯繫，用另一個例子示範。稍後讓學生就「自行選擇閱讀內容」寫作，並減少協助。

    Gu: feedback leads to action; later evidence helps examine the effect.Gu：由回饋帶來行動；稍後的證據有助檢視效果。
