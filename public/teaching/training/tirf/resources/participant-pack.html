Formative assessment / AfL

Elicit and interpret evidence of learning, then use it to decide how teaching and learning should proceed towards a clear goal. Plan fresh evidence to check progress.

Opening case: a learner chooses the better-supported paragraph, “B, because it is longer.” What does the reason suggest? What else might explain it? Compare equally long relevant and irrelevant examples, discuss the difference, then ask for a new justification.

Three frameworks for formative assessment

Use the same writing goal to connect classroom strategies, feedback and follow-up action.

1. Black & Wiliam (2009): five classroom strategies

Teacher, peer and learner contribute to clarifying goals, establishing current learning and deciding how to move forward. The strategies work together across a lesson, rather than forming five consecutive stages.

The five strategies in our writing task寫作任務中的五項策略

  1. Where are we going?我們要達到甚麼？

    Compare A and B with learners. Agree what counts as a specific relevant example and an explanation of its link to the opinion.

    與學生比較A、B兩段，確認甚麼是具體相關的例子，以及如何解釋例子與觀點的聯繫。

  2. Where are we now?我們目前在哪裏？

    Ask every learner to choose and justify, then improve A. “B, because it is longer” suggests a criterion worth checking, even though the choice is correct.

    要求每位學生選擇、說明理由，再改寫A。「B較長」雖然選對，卻提示我們需要核對學生理解的準則。

  3. What will help close the gap?甚麼回饋有助縮小差距？

    If a draft has an example but no explanation, ask: “How does this example support your opinion?” Give time to revise and inspect the change.

    若初稿有例子但欠缺解釋，可問：「這個例子如何支持你的觀點？」留時間修訂，再查看改動。

  4. How can peers help?同伴可以怎樣協助？

    A partner underlines the example, marks the explanation and asks one question against the criteria. The writer decides how to use that information.

    同伴畫出例子、標記解釋，並依準則提出一個問題；作者據此決定如何修訂。

  5. How can learners take charge?學習者如何掌握自己的學習？

    The writer checks both criteria, explains a revision and chooses a next step. A fresh opinion then tests whether the approach transfers.

    作者依兩項準則自查，解釋修訂並選擇下一步，再以新觀點檢視能否運用同一方法。

Black & Wiliam (2009).

2. Hattie & Timperley (2007): a model of feedback

Choose feedback that connects the goal, current performance and a productive next step. Consider both the question it answers and the level it addresses.

Purpose of feedback回饋的目的

Help close the gap between present understanding or performance and the learning goal.協助縮小目前理解或表現與學習目標之間的差距。

Learners’ responses學習者的回應

Learners may use a better strategy or invest purposeful effort. They may also give up, obscure the goal or settle for less; those responses can reduce the perceived gap without improving learning.學習者可以採用更合適的方法或投入有方向的努力，也可能放棄、模糊目標或降低要求；後者可減少感受到的差距，卻未必促進學習。

Teachers’ support教師的支持

Set clear, suitably challenging goals. Help learners reach them through strategies, instruction and feedback.訂立清晰且具適當挑戰的目標，以策略、教學與回饋支持學生達標。

Three feedback questions三個回饋問題

Feed up

Where am I going?我要達到甚麼？

Clarify the goal and what meeting it looks like.釐清目標，以及達標的具體表現。

“Support your opinion with a specific relevant example and explain the connection.”「以具體相關的例子支持觀點，並解釋兩者的聯繫。」

Feed back

How am I going?我目前的表現如何？

Locate current performance in relation to that goal.依目標判斷目前的表現。

“Your daily-reading example is specific. The reader still needs to know how it supports your opinion.”「你每天閱讀的例子很具體，但讀者仍需知道它如何支持你的觀點。」

Feed forward

Where to next?下一步做甚麼？

Identify a productive way to make further progress.指出有助繼續進步的方法。

“Add a sentence explaining what regular reading makes possible; then check the link.”「加一句解釋經常閱讀能帶來甚麼，再檢查它與觀點的聯繫。」

Each question can address four levels每個問題均可涉及四個層面

Task任務層面

Information about the quality or accuracy of this response.針對這次作答的質素或正確性。

“Your paragraph gives an example, but does not explain its link to the opinion.”「你的段落有例子，但未解釋它與觀點的聯繫。」
Process過程層面

Information about a strategy for approaching the work.針對完成任務所用的方法。

“Ask what the example makes possible. Use that answer to connect it to your opinion.”「問自己：這個例子能帶來甚麼？用答案連結你的觀點。」
Self-regulation自我調節層面

Support for checking, directing and adjusting one’s own learning.支持學生監察、引導及調整自己的學習。

“Check both criteria yourself. Which part needs revision, and what help would you choose?”「自行檢查兩項準則。哪一部分需要修訂？你會選擇甚麼協助？」
Self自我／個人層面

Judgements about the person, often with little information for improving the work.對個人的評價，往往缺乏有助改進作品的資訊。

“You are a brilliant writer!” gives no guidance for adding the missing explanation.「你是出色的作者！」未能指導學生補上欠缺的解釋。

Choose the focus that helps this learner progress. The levels are different feedback targets, rather than a four-step sequence; person-focused praise usually offers little guidance for learning.按學習者的需要選擇回饋焦點。四個層面指不同回饋對象，並非四個依次進行的步驟；只評價個人的讚賞，通常難以提供學習指引。

Hattie & Timperley (2007), Figure 1, p. 87; feedback levels, pp. 90–97.

3. Gu (2021): a goal-centred cycle of formative practice

The learning target connects four practices. A complete event includes feedback being taken up through learner and/or teacher action.

1. Eliciting evidence

Create an opportunity to reveal learning against the target.

In our writing task: Ask everyone to choose A or B, explain why and improve A. The reason “because it is longer” adds information that the correct choice alone cannot provide.

2. Interpreting evidence

Judge what the response suggests about the target; consider other explanations.

In our writing task: The learner may be using length as a shortcut. Check with equally long examples before concluding that they cannot judge relevance; the instructions may also need clarification.

3. Formative feedback

Use that interpretation to offer information that supports progress.

In our writing task: After the learner writes, ask: “What makes your example relevant to this opinion?” If the link is missing, prompt an explanation rather than supplying a finished paragraph.

4. Learner/teacher take-up and action

Learners and/or teachers act on the feedback to support learning.

In our writing task: The learner revises and explains the change. The teacher may model a link, give targeted practice or extend the task. Check later with a fresh opinion and less support.

Purpose, practice and effect: intending to help learning, completing the assessment cycle and showing a learning benefit are distinct. One cycle may span lessons; progress may require several cycles. Check a fresh response to examine what learners can do with less support.

Gu (2021), Figure 1 and “Classroom-Based Formative Assessment”.

Use all three in your design brief: plan who contributes evidence and support; choose feedback linked to the goal; specify learner/teacher action and how to check what follows.

Evidence and Explanation Activity: paper version

Suppose intermediate English learners state opinions but struggle to support them. Goal: support an opinion with a specific relevant example and explain its link.

  1. A specific example is relevant to the claim.
  2. An explanation shows how the example supports the claim.

Claim: Students should have a short reading period every school day.

Sample A

Reading every day is good. It is useful and important. Students will become better because reading helps them.

Sample B

A short reading period can help students build a reading habit. For example, learners could finish one short article each day and record two unfamiliar words. This regular practice would give them repeated opportunities to meet vocabulary in context.

Choose a sample and justify it with the criteria. Identify its example and explanation.
